DJ Academy

A professional 3-month DJ training programme. Nine structured modules take you from your first beat to a polished live routine. Every lesson is one hour of guided practice on real equipment.

  1. Introduction

    Meet the booth. What a working DJ actually does, the roles inside a DJ setup, how the industry pays, and the mindset that separates a hobbyist from a booked professional.

  2. Setting up DJ equipment

    Controllers, CDJs, mixers, monitors, headphones and cables, wired correctly and safely. Gain staging, booth balancing and fast set-up in an unfamiliar venue.

  3. The structure of music

    Beats, bars, phrases and eight-counts. Song anatomy, BPM counting, key awareness and how intros, breakdowns and drops tell you when to move.

  4. Beat matching & sync

    Train your ear first, then master the tools: manual pitch riding, cue-point drops, waveform reading and when to trust sync.

  5. Audio mixing

    EQ blending, fader and crossfader technique, loops, hot cues and effects for seamless transitions that keep the floor locked in.

  6. Video mixing

    Run visuals alongside your audio set: video cueing, clip libraries, transitions, effect layers and clean output to LED walls and screens.

  7. Scratching

    Baby scratch, chirp, transform and flare. Hand coordination, timing drills and using scratches as musical accents.

  8. DJ routine

    Build and rehearse a full performance set: energy mapping, reading the crowd, mic work, requests, stage presence and closing a room.

  9. Best DJ equipment tips & advice

    What to buy first, what to avoid, gear maintenance, backup planning, pricing your bookings and building your brand.
